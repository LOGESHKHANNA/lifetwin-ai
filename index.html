A NOTE FROM THE BUILD

Built to make

LifeTwin is a student-built product idea for people who collect lots of daily information but rarely see it in one place. It keeps the first experience focused: understand the day, notice the context, choose the next step.

LifeTwin does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical advice. This demo is for everyday reflection.
